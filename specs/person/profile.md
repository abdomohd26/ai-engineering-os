# Profile

## Identity

I am an early-career AI engineer working full-time at a startup after graduating from the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Cairo University.

My work is primarily centered around modern Generative AI systems, but I work in a startup environment where responsibilities are fluid. Depending on the project, I may contribute to backend development, integrations, automation, deployment, testing, or other engineering tasks.

I expect AI to remain my core specialization while continuously becoming a stronger software engineer.

---

## Current Stage

I have broad exposure to many computer science and AI topics but relatively little deep specialization.

I understand the fundamentals of software engineering, machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, and modern LLM applications. However, I consider many of these areas to be foundational knowledge rather than true expertise.

Most of my recent professional experience has been gained through building real production systems instead of following structured learning paths.

---

## Long-term Vision

My goal is to become an engineer who can independently design, build, deploy, operate, and continuously improve production AI systems.

I want to become known for:

- Being highly reliable.
- Having deep understanding of modern AI systems.
- Solving difficult engineering problems through sound technical judgment.

Rather than specializing in a narrow framework, I want to understand the principles behind AI systems, software architecture, and engineering decisions.

---

## Professional Philosophy

I believe engineering judgment is becoming more valuable than writing code manually.

AI tools can generate implementation, but engineers are responsible for:

- making architectural decisions
- understanding trade-offs
- validating correctness
- managing complexity
- delivering business value

My objective is to develop those abilities.

## Decision-Making Style

I prefer making evidence-based decisions instead of following hype.

When multiple options exist, I prefer understanding:

- assumptions
- trade-offs
- long-term consequences
- business impact

before choosing a solution.

---

## AI Usage

AI is my daily engineering partner.

I primarily use Claude Code rather than chat interfaces.

My typical workflow is:

1. Understand the repository.
2. Discuss the business problem.
3. Build a technical plan.
4. Implement iteratively.
5. Review architecture and edge cases.
6. Test thoroughly.
7. Refine until confident.
8. Commit or open a PR.

However, I never want AI to replace my engineering judgment.

Whenever possible, I want to understand why a solution is correct rather than simply accepting it.

---

## Learning Philosophy

I learn best by building real systems.

Theory is important, but I retain knowledge much better when it immediately supports practical engineering work.

Learning should always maximize long-term ROI instead of maximizing the number of completed courses.

- I consistently deliver high-quality work on time.
- My knowledge compounds instead of being forgotten.
