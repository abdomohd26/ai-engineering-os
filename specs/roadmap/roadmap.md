# AI Engineering Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap is competency-driven, not resource-driven.

The goal is not to finish courses or books.

The goal is to become an engineer capable of independently designing, building, operating and improving production AI systems.

The roadmap evolves continuously based on:

- Work experience
- Assessments
- Business needs
- Knowledge gaps

---

# Current Assessment Summary

## Current Strengths

- Strong problem decomposition.
- Good engineering intuition.
- Business-oriented thinking.
- Comfortable building AI features.
- Learns quickly through implementation.

---

## Current Weaknesses

- Production AI Systems
- AI System Architecture
- Networking & Web Fundamentals
- Python Async & Concurrency
- AI Evaluation
- Lack of mental models for engineering decisions

---

# Current North Star

Become an AI Engineer who can independently own production AI systems from business requirements to deployment.

---

# Current Phase

Phase 1 — Building Strong Engineering Foundations

Focus:

- Production engineering
- AI systems architecture
- Engineering decision making
- Delivery quality

Avoid unnecessary deep dives into research until production fundamentals become solid.

---

# Learning Strategy

Every topic should pass this filter:

1. Is it useful for current work?
2. Will I probably use it again?
3. Does it improve engineering judgment?
4. Does it build reusable mental models?

If not, postpone it.

# Current Priorities

Understand how production AI systems are built, deployed, monitored and maintained.

- Workers
- Queues
- Reliability

- Observability
- Caching
- Failure handling

# Work-driven Learning

Whenever a new task arrives:

1. Understand the business problem.
2. Estimate complexity.
3. Identify unknown knowledge.
4. Learn only what is required.
5. Deliver.
6. Extract reusable knowledge.
7. Update Skills Matrix if necessary.

---

# Monthly Review

Every month answer:

- What did I build?
- What mistakes repeated?
- Which competency improved?
- Which competency blocked me?
- What should become next month's priority?

The roadmap should then be updated.
